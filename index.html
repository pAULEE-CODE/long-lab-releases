Extra contract curve

Drag the amber/pink handles with a mouse or touchscreen, or use the X/Y sliders. Handle changes select Custom Bézier automatically. Base required additions are independent of this curve; requested, accepted and blocked extras remain separate.

Journey stages

Advanced journey controls

Creates a separate journey carrying actual contracts, wallet and unrealized P&L. A contribution is external collateral applied after the checkpoint row and before the next phase; it creates no P&L or contracts. The source journey stays unchanged. Earlier rows in this fork are frozen.

Resume/create waypoint may apply permitted base or explicit catch-up additions at this mark. Settings-only mode marks P&L on existing holdings, makes no additions at this mark, and applies the updated strategy from the next mark.

The Lev tooltip distinguishes actual effective leverage, configured maximum, and the maximum additional capacity available before that mark's orders. Capacity is an upper bound: requested base, extra and catch-up orders remain separate and unused capacity is never filled automatically.

Close Contracts

FIFO lot allocation. The position is marked to market first; a close changes wallet/unrealized classification, adds no equity, and suppresses all additions at this price stage.
